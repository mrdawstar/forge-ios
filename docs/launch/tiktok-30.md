# Forge — 30 short videos

For TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. 7–20 seconds each, filmable by
one person with an iPhone: screen recordings (Control Centre → Screen
Recording), a second phone or a tripod for hands-on-phone shots, and daylight.

## Rules for every video

- **Open on the sword.** The first frame is the sword in the stone or a hand on
  the grip wherever practical. The pull is the thing nobody else has.
- **Say what it is:** a daily practice where you earn the day. Never describe
  Forge as a list app or anything with "tracker" in it.
- **Only real screens.** Record the actual app. No mocked numbers, no staged
  "day 300" accounts presented as yours, no fake comments, reviews, downloads
  or user counts, no promises of results.
- **Voice:** calm, short, no exclamation marks, no countdowns or "don't break the
  chain" pressure.
- **Activation-dependent** videos (marked **[AI]**) show the model-written Weekly
  Reading. Film and post them **only after remote AI is activated**; until then
  the free weekly observation is what the app shows. Everything else works in
  the current build.
- Captions: sentence case, no emoji walls. Hashtags: 3–5, from the pool below.

Hashtag pool: `#discipline` `#dailyroutine` `#selfdiscipline` `#morningroutine`
`#studytok` `#gymtok` `#buildinpublic` `#indiedev` `#iosapp` `#productivity`
`#routine` `#stoic` `#journaling`

The sword coming out of the stone is the product. These show it.

- **On-screen text:** "You don't tick the day off." / "You pull it out."
- **Audio:** the app's own sound, no music; a low room tone.
- **Caption:** The day is banked when you pull it free.
- **Hashtags:** #discipline #dailyroutine #iosapp
