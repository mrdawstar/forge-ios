# Forge — micro-creator outreach

Target: creators with roughly **5k–50k followers** whose audience already cares
about keeping a practice — self-discipline, training, studying. Small enough to
read their DMs, big enough that a genuine mention is seen.

**The offer, exactly:** a free year of Forge Pro through an **Apple Offer Code**.
Nothing else. There is no payment, no sponsorship, no obligation to post, no
script, and no request for a positive review or rating.

This file deliberately contains no list of named creators. Build that list by
hand, using the checklist at the end, in a separate pass.

---

## Before you send anything

- **Offer codes are for the annual subscription.** Create a one-year free offer
  code for `com.dawid.forge.premium.annual` in App Store Connect (Subscriptions
  → Offer Codes). Tell the creator plainly that it **renews at the normal price
  after the year unless they cancel**, and how to cancel. Never let them find
  that out from a charge.
- **One code per person**, recorded in your own sheet (who, when, which code).
- **Disclosure:** if a creator does post about Forge, they received something of
  value from you. Ask them to disclose it the way their platform and local rules
  require (for example a "gifted" label or #gifted / #ad as appropriate). Never
  ask them to hide it.
- **Do not** ask for App Store ratings or reviews, offer anything in exchange for
  one, or suggest what they should say.
- Only send to creators who have not asked for no DMs, and stop after one
  follow-up.

## What *not* to say, in any variant

- Anything about AI being in the app **until remote AI is activated** — then the
  Weekly Reading can be mentioned as a Pro feature that runs only after the user
  allows it.
- "Sponsored", "partnership", "campaign" — there isn't one.
- Download numbers, ratings, how many creators have joined, or quotes from other
  creators.
- Outcomes: "changed my life", "you'll finally be disciplined", health or
  therapy claims.
- Cloud sync, accounts, friends, groups or shared challenges — Forge has none.

## Manual checklist before contacting a creator

Go through their last ~20 posts yourself.

- [ ] **Size:** roughly 5k–50k followers, and engagement that looks human
      (comments that respond to the post, not generic emoji strings).
- [ ] **Fit:** they post about routines, discipline, training or studying
      regularly — not once.
- [ ] **Tone:** calm or serious rather than hustle-shouting; no "buy this or
      you'll fail" energy. Forge's voice is restrained, and theirs should not
      clash with it.
- [ ] **Honesty:** they label gifted/sponsored content when they post it.
- [ ] **No red flags:** no medical or diet claims, no fake giveaways, no
      bought-follower signs (sudden jumps, mismatched comments).
- [ ] **iPhone:** they film on or talk about iPhone — Forge is iPhone only.
- [ ] **Audience:** plausibly English-speaking (the app is English, US-first)
      and not primarily children.
- [ ] **Open DMs**, and no note in their bio asking brands not to message.
- [ ] **Personal line ready:** you can name one real post of theirs in the DM.
- [ ] **Logged:** name, handle, platform, date contacted, code sent, follow-up
      date — in your own sheet.
